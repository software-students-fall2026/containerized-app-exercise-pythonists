# Containerized App Exercise

## Filler Word Coach

Filler Word Coach helps you become a more confident speaker. Record a practice talk in your browser and the app will transcribe your speech, count filler words like "um," "uh," "like," and "basically," measures how fast you speak, and finds long pauses. Every practice session is saved, so you can watch your filler rate and pace improve over time on a dashboard.
